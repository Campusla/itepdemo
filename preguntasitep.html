Pregunta 1 de 30

1. The students ______ to visit a museum tomorrow.

¡Correcto! El sujeto plural exige "are going".

2. She has lived in this city ______ five years.

¡Correcto! "For" se usa con periodos de tiempo.

3. If I ______ rich, I would travel around the world.

¡Correcto! Segundo condicional usa "were".

4. Neither the teacher nor the students ______ present at the meeting.

¡Correcto! Concuerda con el sujeto más cercano.

5. He speaks English ______ than his brother.

¡Correcto! Adverbio comparativo.

6. By this time next year, I ______ my university degree.

¡Correcto! Futuro perfecto.

7. This is the best movie ______ I have ever seen.

¡Correcto! Se prefiere "that" tras superlativo.

8. I look forward to ______ you soon.

¡Correcto! Gerundio tras "look forward to".

9. The report needs ______ before submission.

¡Correcto! Sentido pasivo con gerundio.

10. Scarcely had I arrived home ______ it started to rain.

¡Correcto! Correlación "scarcely... when".

11. Despite ______ hard, she failed the exam.

¡Correcto! Preposición "despite" con gerundio.

12. Not only ______ late, but he also forgot his documents.

¡Correcto! Inversión con "Not only".

13. I wish I ______ more time to finish this project.

¡Correcto! Pasado simple con "I wish".

14. The new software ______ by the IT team next week.

¡Correcto! Voz pasiva en futuro.

15. She suggested ______ to the meeting earlier.

¡Correcto! Gerundio tras "suggest".

16. You ______ have told me about the schedule change!

¡Correcto! "Should have" para reproche.

17. Hard ______ he tried, he couldn't solve the puzzle.

¡Correcto! Estructura concesiva.

18. He acts as if he ______ everything about the business.

¡Correcto! Pasado simple con "as if".

19. Little ______ that the decision would change everything.

¡Correcto! Inversión con "Little".

20. The manager insisted that the report ______ submitted immediately.

¡Correcto! Subjuntivo obligatorio.

21. It's high time you ______ studying for the certification.

¡Correcto! Pasado simple con "It's high time".

22. Supposing you ______ the scholarship, where would you live?

¡Correcto! Condicional hipotético con "supposing".

23. I'd rather you ______ use your phone during the conference.

¡Correcto! Pasado simple con "I'd rather + sujeto".

24. No sooner had we stepped outside ______ the phone rang.

¡Correcto! Correlación "No sooner... than".

25. She is said ______ one of the best programmers in the region.

¡Correcto! Pasiva impersonal con infinitivo.

26. Try ______ quiet when you enter the library.

¡Correcto! "Try to + verbo" para esfuerzo.

27. I remember ______ the document before leaving the office.

¡Correcto! "Remember + gerundio" para recuerdo pasado.

28. So fast ______ that nobody could catch him.

¡Correcto! Inversión con "So + adverbio".

29. The project was successfully completed ______ the tight deadline.

¡Correcto! "Despite" seguido de frase nominal.

30. Had I known about the traffic, I ______ another route.

¡Correcto! Tercer condicional con inversión.
